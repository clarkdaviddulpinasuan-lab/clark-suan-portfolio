Zaman Watch

A high-end horology marketplace built on Shopify, anchored by a custom 3D product visualizer that lets collectors inspect every timepiece before they buy.

The brief

Zaman needed to move from a static catalog to a marketplace that could carry the weight of five-figure timepieces. Trust, detail, and tactility were non-negotiable — buyers expected to scrutinize a watch the way they would under a jeweler's loupe.

The existing theme flattened every product into the same grid, stripped the imagery of context, and buried specifications three clicks deep. Conversion on high-value SKUs was effectively zero.

The approach

I rebuilt the product experience around inspection. A custom Liquid section drives a 360° visualizer with deep-zoom on the dial, case, and movement, paired with a specification ledger that reads like a certificate of authenticity.

  • 360° custom product visualizer with deep-zoom hotspots
  • Structured metafields for movement, complications, and provenance
  • Editorial PDP layout that scales from mobile to desktop
  • Lazy-loaded assets to keep the experience fast despite heavy imagery

Feature highlights

360° Visualizer

Custom canvas viewer with drag-to-rotate and pinch-zoom, built entirely in the theme.

Spec Ledger

Metafield-driven detail panel that renders provenance, movement, and materials cleanly.

Concierge Checkout

High-touch flow with reserve, enquire, and full-purchase paths for different buyer intents.
