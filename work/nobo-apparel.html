The brief

Nobo lives and dies by the drop. The brand needed a storefront that could handle traffic spikes on release day without feeling frantic — clean, fast, and confident enough to let a single garment hold the whole screen.

Their previous theme leaned on clutter: badges, banners, and pop-ups fighting for attention. It read as noisy, not premium.

The approach

I stripped the interface back to type, product, and negative space, then engineered the flow so buying takes as few taps as possible — quick-add from any grid, a slide-out cart, and an express checkout path.

  • Mobile-first grid with quick-add and slide-out cart drawer
  • Drop countdown & back-in-stock capture built in Liquid
  • Editorial lookbook sections that double as PDP entry points
  • Sub-second perceived load via skeletons and image priorities

Feature highlights

Drop Engine

Scheduled countdowns and stock capture that turn release day into an event.

Quick-Add Grid

Add-to-cart without leaving the collection — fewer taps, more throughput.

Lookbook PDPs

Editorial spreads that flow straight into product pages without a hard cut.
