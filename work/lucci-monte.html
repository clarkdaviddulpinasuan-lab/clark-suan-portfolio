The brief

Lucci Monte wanted their storefront to feel like the print campaigns they were already famous for — considered, immersive, and unmistakably editorial. The challenge was doing that inside Shopify without sacrificing performance or merchandising control.

Off-the-shelf themes forced their product photography into rigid grids that killed the mood the brand had spent years building.

The approach

I built a library of modular editorial sections — full-bleed spreads, split narratives, and scroll-linked reveals — that the team can compose per collection from the theme editor, no code required.

  • Composable editorial sections editable from the Shopify customizer
  • Scroll-linked GSAP reveals tuned to respect reduced-motion
  • Art-directed responsive imagery for phone, tablet, and desktop
  • Merchandising-friendly: every spread still routes to a live PDP

Feature highlights
